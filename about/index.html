About us

We are redesigning how medicine is learned.

Pergamed is a medical education and technology brand born in Anatolia. We bring clinical content, software and artificial intelligence into a single structure, and rebuild the way physicians learn, plan and work.

Why Pergamed?

Our name comes from one of the oldest centres of medical knowledge.

I

Pergamon · Bergama

A hill in Anatolia, above today's Bergama in İzmir: Pergamon. With its library and its healing sanctuary, it was one of the great centres of learning in the ancient world.

II

Asklepion

Below the city lay the Asklepion, a healing sanctuary where patients sought care and physicians gathered. Galen, born in Pergamon, learned medicine here among those physicians, and knowledge passed from physician to physician.

III

Parchment

Parchment, the material used to record knowledge, was refined in this city until both sides of a leaf could be written on. Its name still carries the city's: pergamena in Latin.

IV

Pergamon + med

We chose the name Pergamed because it recalls the medical heritage of Bergama and its Asklepion. “Med” says medicine, and the name is easy to say in Turkish and in English. From Anatolia's medical heritage to today's physician.

What we do

Medical knowledge has never grown faster, yet the way it is learned has barely changed. Pergamed exists to close that gap.

Depth in a single app

Each of our apps brings a specialty's textbook knowledge, question bank, diagrams and tools into one place. Our first app, Sutura, is for plastic surgery; emergency medicine, pediatrics and internal medicine are on our roadmap.

Scale through AI

We prepare content with AI assistance, check it and keep it current. That lets a comprehensive curriculum grow far faster than traditional publishing allows.

From training to practice

Today we focus on specialty training. Next, we will build tools that help physicians run their practice and stay connected with their patients.

Global from day one

Every product is designed for international use and released in English first.

Our apps are developed by physicians. All of them are educational tools; they do not give medical advice and do not replace clinical judgement.
