Support

Questions, suggestions and bug reports are welcome. Write to support@pergamed.com; we reply within a few working days.

When reporting a problem, include the app name, your phone model and operating system version, and a screenshot if you can. Please never send patient information.

Common questions

How do I cancel or change my subscription?

Subscriptions are managed by Apple or Google. On iPhone: Settings → your name → Subscriptions. On Android: Google Play → profile → Payments & subscriptions → Subscriptions. Cancelling stops the next renewal; you keep access until the end of the paid period.

How do I get a refund?

Refunds are decided by the store you paid through. Request one at reportaproblem.apple.com for the App Store, or from your Google Play order history for Android.

I changed phones. How do I get Pro back?

Sign in with the same Apple or Google account and use Restore purchases in the app. Your logbook is stored on the old phone; export it from the Account screen before you switch.

How do I download or delete my data?

In Sutura, open the Account screen. Download my data exports everything as a single file; Delete account erases your server account. Details are in the privacy policy.

I found an error in a topic or question.

Thank you. Send the topic or question title and what you think is wrong to support@pergamed.com. Corrections are reviewed and included in the next content update.

Can I use Sutura for clinical decisions?

No. Sutura is an educational tool. It does not replace clinical judgement and must not be used on its own for diagnosis, treatment or dosing.
