Sutura – Plastic Surgery.

A study and residency companion for plastic, reconstructive and aesthetic surgery residents, from hand surgery and microsurgery to craniofacial, aesthetic and breast surgery.

What's inside

Topics
Structured summaries for each condition: definition, diagnosis, treatment and cases, with diagrams and links to sources.
Question bank and exam mode
Practice by topic, or sit a timed exam and review every answer with its explanation.
Diagrams
Classic schematics and planning visuals placed next to the text they explain.
Study tools
Nine study and reference tools in one place, with more to come. They are for learning and quick reference, not for treatment decisions.
Logbook
One of the nine tools: record your cases and on-call shifts. Entries stay on your phone; never enter anything that identifies a patient.
Congresses and journals
Upcoming congress dates and abstract deadlines, and the latest issues of the journals you follow.
