Why Sutura?

Sutura

Sutura is Latin for “seam”; it comes from the verb “to sew”, to join parts together. In surgery it is the stitch that closes a wound; in anatomy, the sutures that join the bones of the skull. In plastic and craniofacial surgery, both meanings come up every day.

Bringing the parts together

Sutura brings together, in one app, the parts a plastic surgery resident needs. The three words at the top of the app name them.

Learn

More than 300 topic summaries, over 2,500 questions with explanations, more than 900 diagrams and figures, and mock exams in board format.

Plan

A personal study plan built around your exam date, an on-call planner, congress dates and the latest issues of the journals you follow.

Operate

A logbook for the operations you take part in, and quick reference tools: burn formulas, lidocaine dosing, melanoma staging and botulinum toxin reconstitution. All tools are for education.

A companion

Our aim is to ease the resident's path to the operating room: to be a companion at every step of training.
