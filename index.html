How we build

Every Pergamed app is planned by a physician in its specialty and held to the same three rules.

Grounded in the textbooks

Topics and questions rest on established reference knowledge. Recent articles are cited for context, with links to the source.

Physician-led, AI-assisted

Content is prepared with AI assistance under physician supervision, so it can grow quickly without losing clinical sense.

Private by default

No advertising and no tracking. Personal records such as the logbook stay on the device. See the privacy policy.

One company, many specialties

Pergamed starts with the years of specialty training: one app per field, written for the way residents study between cases and on-call shifts. Plastic surgery comes first; dermatology, internal medicine and pediatrics follow.

Over time we will widen from education to tools that help physicians run their practice and stay connected with their patients. Every product is built for an international audience and released in English first.

Our apps are study and practice tools. They do not give medical advice and do not replace clinical judgement.
